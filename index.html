Who We Are

Company Profile

TRIPPLE Z ENGINEERING SERVICES was founded by its General Manager, who began his career as a Machinist and Marketing Sales professional in a reputed Mold Maker and Metal Fabrication company in the Philippines in 2004. Over the years, he gained vast experience in operations and successfully executed many large-scale projects in metal fabrication, automation, semiconductor mold, and mechanical services.

In September 2020, the business was established as Z & Z DHAGZ ENTERPRISES — Non-Specialized Wholesale Trading. Driven by growth and opportunity, the company expanded into Metal Fabrication and Metal Machining under the name Tripple Z Engineering Services.

Today, we provide Metal Fabrication Services and Fabrication of Automation Industrial Equipment, serving Industrial and Food Companies across the Philippines with precision, reliability, and a commitment to quality workmanship.
